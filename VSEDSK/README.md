# VSEDSK - Video Synthesis Ecosphere Desktop
Names and versions of Andrei Jay's desktop instruments. Links and videos are in the [main README](/README.md#vsedsk-video-synthesis-ecosphere-desktop).

## Names

| Name | Short name | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| [Waaave Pool DSK Pro](https://videosynthesisecosphere.com/waaave-pool-dsk-pro) | WPDSK Pro, WPDSKPRO | Semi-modular video synthesizer with a new engine. Free, Basic, and Advanced versions |
| [Gravity Waaaves DSK](https://videosynthesisecosphere.com/gravity-waaaves-dsk) | GWDSK | Paid 2 channel video mixer and dual video delay line. Desktop port of the VSEJET GRAVITY_WAAAVES |
| [Waaave Pool DSK](https://videosynthesisecosphere.com/waaave-pool-dsk) | WPDSK | Free desktop port of the VSERPI WAAAVE_POOL |
| [VIDEO_WAAAVES](https://andreijaycreativecoding.com/video-synthesis-ecosphere-DSKP-legacy) | VW, VSEDSKP | Legacy. The original open source desktop suite (2.5, 2.718), built from source with openFrameworks |

- WPDSK and WPDSK Pro Free are different programs.
- Names without "DSK" are the hardware versions.
- On older pages, VSEDSK means VIDEO_WAAAVES. [Andrei explains the history](https://andreijaycreativecoding.com/video-synthesis-ecosphere-DSKP-legacy).

## WPDSK Pro versions
Features of each version, from the [WPDSK Pro page](https://videosynthesisecosphere.com/waaave-pool-dsk-pro). The v0.2.0 beta may not have all of them.

| | Free | Basic | Advanced |
|---|---|---|---|
| Source plugins | Fixed chain | 4 slots | 8 slots |
| Delay plugins | Fixed chain | 4 slots | 8 slots |
| Modulation sources | - | 1 video oscillator, 1 general video source, 4 LFOs | 2 video oscillators, 2 general video sources, 8 LFOs |
| Resolution and frame rate | 800x600 at 60fps | Up to 1920x1080 at 60fps | Up to what your system can handle |
| Delay length | 1 second | Customizable | Customizable |
| Presets | - | Per plugin, per channel, and global. Small selection included | Same as Basic, with many more included |
| Video loops | - | Small selection | Many |
| MIDI | Coming | Coming | Coming |
