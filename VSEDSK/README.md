# VSEDSK - Video Synthesis Ecosphere Desktop
VSEDSK is the umbrella name for Andrei Jay's desktop instruments. The name stands for "Video Synthesis Ecosphere DeSKtop" ([FAQ](https://videosynthesisecosphere.com/faq)). Links and videos for each instrument are in the [main README](/README.md#vsedsk-video-synthesis-ecosphere-desktop).

## Names

| Name | Short name | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| [Waaave Pool DSK Pro](https://videosynthesisecosphere.com/waaave-pool-dsk-pro) | WPDSK Pro, WPDSKPRO | Semi-modular video synthesizer with a new engine. Free, Basic, and Advanced versions |
| [Gravity Waaaves DSK](https://videosynthesisecosphere.com/gravity-waaaves-dsk) | GWDSK | Paid 2 channel video mixer and dual video delay line. Desktop port of the VSEJET GRAVITY_WAAAVES |
| [Waaave Pool DSK](https://videosynthesisecosphere.com/waaave-pool-dsk) | WPDSK | Free desktop port of the VSERPI WAAAVE_POOL |
| [VIDEO_WAAAVES](https://andreijaycreativecoding.com/video-synthesis-ecosphere-DSKP-legacy) | VW, VSEDSKP | Legacy. The original open source desktop suite (2.5, 2.718), built from source with openFrameworks |

- WPDSK and WPDSK Pro are different programs. Pro has a new synthesis engine, and its Free version is not the same download as WPDSK.
- WAAAVE_POOL without "DSK" is the VSERPI hardware instrument, and WAAAVE_POOL HD is the VSEJET one.
- On older pages "VSEDSK" and "VSEDSKP" mean the VIDEO_WAAAVES suite. Andrei explains that history in the [notes on nomenclature](https://andreijaycreativecoding.com/video-synthesis-ecosphere-DSKP-legacy).

## Support and community notes
- [Support form and community links](https://videosynthesisecosphere.com/support)
- [GWDSK and WPDSK community notes](/GWDSK)
- [GWDSK community help guide](/GWDSK/GWDSK-help.md)
- Spout and Syphon are not supported, and there is no immediate plan to add them ([FAQ](https://videosynthesisecosphere.com/faq)).
- If you have to re-enter your registration every time, or can't save presets, the software can't write to the folder it is installed in. Move the whole folder somewhere else or reinstall it elsewhere ([FAQ](https://videosynthesisecosphere.com/faq)).
