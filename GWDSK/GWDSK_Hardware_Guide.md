## Buyer Verification

**⚠️ Always verify serial numbers before purchase:**

1. Ask seller for device serial (format: `FB8M-9Z`, `M3LS-H1`)
2. Check registration status: `help.steampowered.com/en/accountdata/SteamHardwareRegistrations`
3. Contact Steam Support to verify device isn't stolen/banned
4. Test device rewards redemption (stolen devices will show "serial number can't be recognized")
